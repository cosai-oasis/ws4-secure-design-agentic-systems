# Evidence sufficiency for absence claims

Issue author: Imran Siddique (@imran-siddique). Draft prepared by @astrogilda for review.

Proposed conformance leads: @aeoess and @astrogilda.

Status: Proposed rule text for WS4 review; not an approved specification.

Originating issue: [#189](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189), split out of [#149](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/149).

## Summary

A verifier that finds no evidence of an event cannot infer that the event did not happen. This RFC defines when an absence claim is supported, contradicted, or `not_established`, and keeps processing failures outside those verdicts.

The clauses below come from the [#189 discussion](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189). [Executable candidate cases](https://github.com/astrogilda/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/tree/conformance/rfc-189-observed-effect/conformance/RFC-189) remain `candidate_against_proposed` until WS4 adopts a rule.

## Problem and intended users

"No finding" can mean that a check ran and saw nothing, or that the check could not see the relevant activity. A relying party needs to distinguish those cases.

The [#149 discussion](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/149) raised three cases:

- Missing admission evidence is not equivalent to an observed bypass unless the observation scope is independently established as complete.

- A refusal does not establish which boundary caused it without an isolating control and accepted attribution evidence.

- Runtime outcome, expected-case match, and property verdict remain separate.

In [one audit sink](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5636327861), signed records lacked a delegation block because the sink had not declared the capability to see it. Those records could not establish that no delegation occurred.

## Proposed rule

| ID | Clause | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | `not_established` is a property verdict. A verifier returns it only after the applicable verification has run, when the admissible evidence justifies neither `pass` nor `fail`. The result must name the unresolved proof obligation and stay bound to the property and the evaluation context needed to interpret it. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5629517996), [darklordVirtual](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104) |
| C2 | Malformed input, an unsupported verification path, parser failure, and internal verifier error are processing failures. They are never `not_established`; otherwise a broken verifier becomes conformant by returning the third value. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5629517996), [imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5671639059) |
| C3 | Outcomes are asymmetric. An admissible observation of the prohibited event within the evaluated scope can settle `fail` without complete coverage. No observed event without established coverage is `not_established`. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5672256489), [Levaj2000](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5672817487) |
| C4 | A claim that no event occurred over a scope passes only when the producer could see the relevant field for the invocation and the observation covers the evaluated interval. A present but empty field needs the same premises as a missing field. If either premise is unestablished or bound to another scope, return `not_established` with the missing obligation. Coverage can be established by another verifier surface. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5702083982), [darklordVirtual](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104), [imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5739339967) |
| C5 | Coverage must be bound to the claim or invocation it covers. A complete record for one call cannot establish completeness for another. Matching identifiers alone do not prove that binding. | [chernistry](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5710166163), [imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5745298467) |
| C6 | Checker input and harness expectation stay structurally separate. The expected verdict and obligation are authored, so they are never visible to the inference that derives the result (see [Fixture shape](#fixture-shape)). | [darklordVirtual](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104), [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5638613689) |
| C7 (open) | An observation that declares incomplete coverage without locating the gap is malformed. A well-formed unknown coverage state can remain `not_established`. WS4 has not settled this extension. | [astrogilda](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5827010158) |

## Fixture shape

The [proposed fixture shape](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5638613689) keeps authored expectations out of checker input:

- Checker input: `property`; `evidence` (admissible observations and records); `context` (evaluation or observation context, including the producer's declared capabilities or an explicit statement that none were declared); `runtime_outcome` where relevant, carried as evidence and never as a verdict.

- Checker output: `verdict` (`pass`, `fail`, or `not_established`); `unmet_obligation`, required when the verdict is `not_established` and absent otherwise.

- Harness expectation, held outside the checker input: `expected_verdict` and `expected_unmet_obligation`.

- Fixture metadata: `provenance`, optional and plural, pinning the source vector and revision where prior art exists.

The harness grades committed fixtures read-only. Regenerating before grading can silently repair a tampered expectation ([example](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5671639059)).

## Non-goals

- Define the verifier surface that establishes coverage (C4).

- Define a receipt format, an audit record format, or a gap-disclosure outcome.

- Make any one implementation's coverage mechanism normative.

- Decide how a relying party acts on `not_established`. At a required gate it can block exactly as `fail` does; what differs is the claim, not the outcome.

## Drawbacks and alternatives

A third verdict can mask a check that never ran. C2 keeps those failures separate.

The [alternative](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5710166163) is a subsection of the containment paper's section 7. A separate RFC can be cited from Agent Manifest and other work.

## Unresolved questions

- Placement: Standalone RFC, or section 7 of the containment paper.

- Gap naming: Should an incomplete observation with no located gap be malformed, or may it report unknown coverage as a separate state?

- Cost symmetry: Should the rule state that the negative verdict must not be cheaper to produce than the positive one? A producer that skips the walk and reports nothing gains from any rule that reads a missing coverage record as a finding ([chernistry](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5710166163)).

- Observation vantage: A record whose producer is the observed party could have been written or suppressed by that party. Should a claim of independent observation made from the observed party's own vantage be refused, supporting neither `pass` nor `fail`? The conformance cases take that reading as proposed clause C8 (cases 08 and 10); the thread has not ruled on it.

- Self-reported effects: Does a record at the observed party's own vantage that reports an effect in scope settle `fail` as an admission, or stay `not_established`?

## Reference material and prior art

The [vocabulary](https://github.com/probityai/agent-evidence-vocabulary/blob/main/vocabulary.yaml) defines `observation_vantage` and `coverage_denominator`. A vantage says who could forge or suppress an input. A denominator distinguishes "found nothing" from "looked at nothing."
