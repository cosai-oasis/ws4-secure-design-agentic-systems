# Evidence sufficiency: what a verifier may conclude when evidence is absent

Author: Imran Siddique (@imran-siddique).

Conformance leads: @aeoess and @astrogilda.

Status: Proposed rule text for WS4 review; not an approved specification.

Originating issue: [#189](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189), split out of [#149](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/149).

## Summary

A verifier that finds no evidence for a property has three possible answers, and many systems implement two. Passing is wrong, because absence is not conformance. Failing is wrong, because absence is not violation. This RFC names the third answer, `not_established`, and states when a verifier may return it and what the result must carry.

The rule below is the text the #189 thread converged on. Each clause links the comments it comes from. Executable candidate cases for it are in [`conformance/RFC-189/`](../conformance/RFC-189/) and stay `candidate_against_proposed` until WS4 adopts the rule.

## Problem and intended users

A relying party reads a verifier's result to decide whether an agent may proceed, whether an incident happened, or whether a control held. When the verifier returns "no finding" for two different reasons (the check ran and saw nothing, or the check could not see the relevant activity), the relying party cannot tell them apart, and whoever reads the result first collapses the third answer into one of the other two.

The #149 discussion produced three concrete cases:

- Missing admission evidence is not equivalent to an observed bypass unless the observation scope is independently established as complete.

- A refusal does not establish which boundary caused it without an isolating control and accepted attribution evidence.

- Runtime outcome, expected-case match, and property verdict remain separate.

The same collapse appears one layer below the verifier. An audit sink that declared no capabilities emitted records that still chained and still signed while carrying no delegation block, so "no delegation occurred" and "the sink was not permitted to see it" produced identical evidence ([Levaj2000](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5636327861)).

## Proposed rule

| ID | Clause | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | `not_established` is a property verdict. A verifier returns it only after the applicable verification has run, when the admissible evidence justifies neither `pass` nor `fail`. The result must name the unresolved proof obligation and stay bound to the property and the evaluation context needed to interpret it. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5629517996), [darklordVirtual](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104) |
| C2 | Malformed input, an unsupported verification path, parser failure, and internal verifier error are processing failures. They are never `not_established`; otherwise a broken verifier becomes conformant by returning the third value. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5629517996), [imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5671639059) |
| C3 | Outcomes are asymmetric. One observed effect inside the evaluated scope settles `fail` without any completeness premise. No observed effect, without independently established coverage, is `not_established`. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5672256489), [Levaj2000](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5672817487) |
| C4 | A property stating that no relevant event occurred over a scope passes only when two premises are established for that same scope: field visibility (producer capability coverage) and observation completeness (observation coverage). An empty field needs the same checks as a missing one. If either premise is missing, not established, or bound to a different scope, the verdict is `not_established` and names the missing premise. Where coverage has to be established, a separate verifier surface establishes it, and this verifier consumes the resulting bounded context. It does not establish coverage recursively. | [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5702083982), [darklordVirtual](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104), [imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5739339967) |
| C5 | Coverage is bound to the claim or invocation it covers. A complete record for one tool call cannot establish completeness for another, and matching identifiers alone do not establish that link. | [chernistry](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5710166163), [imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5745298467) |
| C6 | Checker input and harness expectation stay structurally separate. The expected verdict and obligation are authored, so they are never visible to the inference that derives the result (see [Fixture shape](#fixture-shape)). | [darklordVirtual](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104), [aeoess](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5638613689) |
| C7 | An admission of incomplete coverage that does not name the gap is malformed input, under C2. A `not_established` result must name its obligation, and an unnamed gap leaves nothing to name. | [astrogilda](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5827010158) |

## Fixture shape

Frozen in [aeoess's revision](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5638613689) after [darklordVirtual's two refinements](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5630431104) and [Levaj2000's producer-side addition](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5636327861).

- Checker input: `property`; `evidence` (admissible observations and records); `context` (evaluation or observation context, including the producer's declared capabilities or an explicit statement that none were declared); `runtime_outcome` where relevant, carried as evidence and never as a verdict.

- Checker output: `verdict` (`pass`, `fail`, or `not_established`); `unmet_obligation`, required when the verdict is `not_established` and absent otherwise.

- Harness expectation, held outside the checker input: `expected_verdict` and `expected_unmet_obligation`.

- Fixture metadata: `provenance`, optional and plural, pinning the source vector and revision where prior art exists.

A conforming harness grades committed fixtures read-only. A runner that regenerates fixtures before grading them can silently repair the artifact it is checking ([imran-siddique](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5671639059)).

## Relationship to adjacent work

| Work | Relationship to this RFC |
| --- | --- |
| Containment paper, section 7 (`feat/containment`, merged in #184) | States the same boundary for evidence records: missing visibility or incomplete observation is `not_established` with the missing premise, and malformed records stay visible as processing failures. This RFC supplies the verdict rule that section cites. |
| `agentrust-io/trace-spec#260`, `receipt_gap_disclosed` | An action-receipt outcome for a signed gap disclosure in a receipt chain. Accepting a disclosed gap is verifier policy, and it cannot satisfy a profile that requires independently proven completeness. It stays a separate layer from the property verdict. |
| RFC-149, Agent Manifest (#210) | Its runtime evidence row points here for "Declared versus observed state, observation completeness, and what links a decision to an effect." |
| `conformance/RFC-189/` | Two candidate case sets for this rule: producer audit records exercising field visibility and observation coverage, and signed Observed Effect records exercising observation coverage and observation vantage. |

## Non-goals

- Define the verifier surface that establishes coverage (C4).

- Define a receipt format, an audit record format, or a gap-disclosure outcome.

- Make any one implementation's coverage mechanism normative.

- Decide how a relying party acts on `not_established`. At a required gate it can block exactly as `fail` does; what differs is the claim, not the outcome.

## Drawbacks and alternatives

A third verdict can be misused as a default for any check that is hard to finish. C2 and C7 exist to close that path: a verifier that did not run, or a producer that did not say where it was blind, gets a processing failure, not a verdict.

The alternative weighed in the thread is a subsection of the containment paper's section 7 ([chernistry](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5710166163)). That placement keeps the rule beside its first consumer. A standalone RFC keeps it citable by RFC-149 and by later work outside containment.

## Unresolved questions

- Placement: Standalone RFC, or section 7 of the containment paper.

- Cost symmetry: Should the rule state that the negative verdict must not be cheaper to produce than the positive one? A producer that skips the walk and reports nothing gains from any rule that reads a missing coverage record as a finding ([chernistry](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/189#issuecomment-5710166163)).

- Observation vantage: A record whose producer is the observed party could have been written or suppressed by that party. Should a claim of independent observation made from the observed party's own vantage be refused, supporting neither `pass` nor `fail`? The conformance cases take that reading as proposed clause C8 (cases 08 and 10); the thread has not ruled on it.

- Self-reported effects: Does a record at the observed party's own vantage that reports an effect in scope settle `fail` as an admission, or stay `not_established`?
