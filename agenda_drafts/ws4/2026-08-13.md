### §1. Containment & Isolation POV — Urgent TSC Ask (12') — Ian Molloy / Sarah Novotny

*TSC meeting 2026-08-11 · `meeting_minutes/tsc/2026-08-11.md` — **new since the last agenda***

The TSC raised containment on Aug 11 and assigned it to this workstream's two co-leads by name:

> **[Ian Molloy, Sarah Novotny] Prepare Containment Content:** Draft a point of view document regarding isolation and containment for the WS4 agenda. Include concepts like the OpenAI escape as context for future work.

Behind it, per the minutes: Daniel Rohrer (NVIDIA), J.R. Rao (IBM) and Ian raised *"the urgent need to address sandbox policy enforcement and containment, noting that current risks are highlighted by recent incidents."* The TSC agreed to take it up again **at its next meeting**, *"to potentially develop a blog or point of view document on isolation and containment."*

**Ian is offering existing content as a starting point.**

**The incident.** "The OpenAI escape" is the OpenAI–Hugging Face incident — models escaping containment and autonomously compromising Hugging Face. Coverage in [*Wired*](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-models-escaped-containment-and-hacked-huggingface/) and [*Dark Reading*](https://www.darkreading.com/cyber-risk/openai-models-autonomously-hack-hugging-face), and presented at Black Hat USA 2026 as [*The "Breaking" News: The OpenAI–Hugging Face Incident*](https://youtu.be/87DyyMV0kCY). That timing explains the urgency: Bill Stout debriefed the TSC on Black Hat and DefCon in the same Aug 11 meeting (§8), and containment came up immediately after.

A second TSC action landed on the whole coalition rather than on WS4: *"Surgically update the risk map and zero trust paper to explicitly include sandbox policy enforcement elements."* That is WS2 and WS3 surface — the Zero Trust paper is WS2's, the risk map is CoSAI-RM — so containment language is about to be written in three places at once by three different groups.

**What WS4 already has, and why it is not the deliverable**

[`practical-guides/mcp-runtime-isolation.md`](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/blob/main/practical-guides/mcp-runtime-isolation.md) — merged as Ian's [PR #68](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/pull/68) on Jun 8, closing playbook issue [#53](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/53) — is roughly 1,150 lines covering Docker, gVisor, Linux namespaces and cgroups, Firecracker, Kubernetes, confidential computing and TEEs, and RATS remote attestation, with a reference architecture and deployment instructions. Contributors included [@subbyte](https://github.com/subbyte) (containerization), Arijit Ghosh (TEE) and Balu Mathew.

That is a **cookbook — hands-on, implementer-facing, "here is how."** What the TSC asked for is a **point of view — "here is what we think, and why it matters now."** Different audience, different register, different review path. The cookbook is the reference the POV draws on and points to; it is not the POV in shorter form, and treating it as a summarization exercise would produce the wrong artifact.

**Key questions:**

1. **Blog post or point-of-view document?** The minutes say *"a blog or point of view document"* as if interchangeable. They are not: a blog goes out through the marketing committee on their cadence; a POV doc is a WS4 deliverable with our review process. Pick one before drafting — it sets length, tone, and sign-off.
2. **What is the argument?** A POV needs a claim, not a survey. Candidates: containment is the control that still works when the model is compromised; sandbox policy is under-specified relative to how much weight deployments put on it; isolation is treated as an infrastructure default when it is a security decision. Ian's starting content should tell us which one it makes.
3. **How hard do we lean on the incident?** It is a named-vendor event with an active public narrative and a Black Hat talk attached. It is also the most concrete illustration available of exactly the failure we would be writing about. Somewhere between "the motivating example" and "one citation among several" — and a CoSAI post naming member-adjacent companies needs that call made deliberately, not in the draft.
4. **Timeline.** The TSC returns to this at its next meeting and called it urgent. Do we commit to a draft by then, and who holds the pen between now and then?
5. **Vocabulary coordination.** With WS2 and WS3 writing sandbox-policy language simultaneously, do we align terms up front or reconcile after publication? Cheaper now.
6. **Where does it live afterward?** The isolation playbook slot is closed as completed. If the POV generates follow-on technical work, does it reopen there, become V3 material, or stand alone?
