### §1. Observability — withdraw the RFC, refile as a control (10') — Emrick Donadei

**Decide: withdraw [#175](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/175) and refile the behavioural half as a control.** This is Emrick's own recommendation, posted 9/1, and it is the cheapest reading of what he found.

The component we were about to propose already exists. [`componentAuditRecordRepository`](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/secure-ai-tooling/blob/develop/risk-map/yaml/components.yaml) — already on `develop` — covers **semantic** observability — *"can we reconstruct what happened."* Decision traces, reasoning logs, delegation-chain events, provenance.

What it does not cover is **behavioural** observability — *"should we intervene?"* Baselines, drift, tool-usage anomaly detection, and the automated response path: circuit breakers, kill switches, escalation gates. In Emrick's words:

> the LLM that I used to pre-write that RFC confused a little bit the need for a second component, it should probably be reframed as a control.

Three consequences worth stating out loud:

1. **The top-level-versus-subcategory question dies here.** It sat on three agendas and was already superseded before it was last asked. [`#501`](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/secure-ai-tooling/pull/501) as written — a component plus two sub-components, plus an edit to `AgentReasoningCore` — is now the wrong shape and should close with the RFC rather than be reworked.
2. **The eight observability risks lose their blocker and gain a different one.** They can attach to `componentAuditRecordRepository` today. They no longer wait on a component PR; they wait on someone drafting them.
3. **Fewer RFCs owed, not more.** The 8/26 reading was three RFCs — decommissioning, observability, reflection-and-memory. Withdrawing one leaves two, and only reflection-and-memory is still unfiled.

**The ask is one comment.** Chairs and the CoSAI-RM SIG confirming the withdrawal and the reframe. That unblocks risk drafting for the Runtime and Reflection phases.

> **Queue warning.** Contributors to the Risk Map are serialised on purpose — *"I can only have so many people in queue to modify the same space at any one time."* WS2's telemetry work is ahead of us. Know that before anyone commits to a date in §4.

### §2. Decommissioning — the phase now has somewhere to land (10') — Bill Stout

**Decide: accept decommissioning as a phase in the definitional paper, and hold the risk catalogue until that paper publishes.**

The thread has converged on Imran Siddique's definition, offered on 9/3:

> Decommissioning **ends the agent's authority to act**; whether it can come back later is a policy choice, and if it comes back it re-enters through Admission as a new entity.

That resolves the naming argument Nicolai Nielsen raised — "retirement" carries the same ambiguity, so the fix was removing the reversible mode, not renaming the phase.

**Where the RFC now stands:**

- ✅ Soft/hard mode is struck through in the body. **Residual cleanup:** "hard decommissioning" and "hard retirement" still appear in the entry criteria and the System Governance row, where they no longer have anything to contrast against. Worth a pass before the PR.
- Bill's remaining ask from 9/3: soft and hard need **organizational** definitions, set locally, in the way NIST SP 800-53 parameters are.
- Imran's substantive changes are not yet reflected: identity **tombstoning** rather than deletion (deleting the identifier breaks replay detection), and a **disposition receipt** that passes when every discovered asset has a verified outcome — revoked, deleted, crypto-shredded, retained to a date, held, or an explicit exception — rather than when everything is deleted.
- Raymond Sheh's addition: what breaks downstream when an agent other systems depend on is retired, and the institutional knowledge that goes with it.

**The sequencing objection is now a dependency, not a disagreement.** David LaBianca's position was that end-of-life risks should not land before a finalized foundational document exists. Putting decommissioning into the definitional paper *as a phase* satisfies that — Bill's framing on 9/3 was *"there is need of a place to land."* The catch is honest and belongs on the table: **that landing place is a document nobody is currently writing.** See §3.

The separate change — `decommissioning` as order 9 in the Risk Map `lifecycle-stage.yaml` — stays on its own track and needs Risk Map owners, not only this SIG. [`#500`](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/secure-ai-tooling/pull/500) is the vehicle and is still a draft.

Also open on [#170](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/170): its third question ties this to a Reflection stage ([`secure-ai-tooling#401`](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/secure-ai-tooling/issues/401)). Recommend keeping them separate.
