### 4. Opinion-shaping format: paper review at the top of the meeting (10 min, Emrick)

**Decide:** run it or not, first piece, first presenter.

The paper is supposed to carry CoSAI opinions, and a risk-table review does not produce opinions. At a recent SF event we opened by discussing Lilian Weng's [*Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement*](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/) to introduce recursive self-improvement, and it produced the best discussion of the day. Proposal:

- **Format.** First 10 to 15 minutes of the meeting. One presenter, one piece, a three-minute summary and **one claim to argue with**. The rest is the room.
- **Output.** Each session ends with a position we either write into §6.2 of [PR #197](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/pull/197) (the positions table, one dated row with its source) or explicitly decline to take. A position nobody writes down did not happen.
- **Cadence.** Every meeting while the paper is in drafting; drop to alternate weeks once prose is landing.
- **Presenter rotation.** Co-leads first, then phase owners.

Candidate pieces, all already cited by [PR #197](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/pull/197) or by this repo, so nobody has to read something new to start:

| Piece | The claim to argue | Paper section it feeds |
|-------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Weng, *Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement* (Jul 2026) | The harness, not the model, is where behavior is determined and where it changes over time. If true, "behavior is fixed at build" fails harder than the draft says, and Maintenance and Reflection are the load-bearing phases | Assumption 1, §3.6, §3.7 |
| Claxton, *The AI-Native SDLC Playbook* (Aug 2026, ref 7 in [PR #197](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/pull/197)) | The most operationally mature guidance treats the agent as the instrument, not the artifact. Is that a gap we fill, or a sign the distinction will not hold? | §1.1 framing, §1.2 |
| CIS *AI Agent Companion Guide* (ref 5, also #130) | An architectural decomposition is enough; a temporal lifecycle is redundant | §1.2, open question 3 |
| CoSAI sandboxing blog ([#167](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/pull/167)) and containment follow-on ([#172](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/issues/172)) | Containment posture is a design-time decision the lifecycle gates, not a runtime mechanism it prescribes | §2.1, §3.1, §3.4 |

Recommend the Weng post first, for 9/23, presented by Emrick, since it has already been road-tested.
